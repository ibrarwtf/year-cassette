YC-01 · Year Cassette

One year.
One roll.

A photo a day, printed on cheap receipt paper and wound into a small box. Crank it to replay the year.

As a wall collage, a year is three metres wide. As framed months, it's twelve frames. So instead, each year is one roll in one box.

Fully mechanical: no battery, no screws. Turn the crank clockwise to play and anticlockwise to rewind; a swing-idler gear picks the spool, like a cassette deck.

Size234 × 84 × 85 mm
Paper58 mm thermal roll
Parts13 prints, no supports
Material~250 g PLA, two colours
