Year Cassette

A whole year, one photo a day, printed on a single roll of cheap receipt paper. Crank it to replay the year.

Thermal paper costs next to nothing, so a year of memories could be one long print. But as a wall collage it's three metres wide, and as framed months it's 12 frames a year. So instead, each year becomes one roll in one small box.

It's fully mechanical, with no electronics and no screws. One crank: clockwise plays, anticlockwise rewinds. A swing-idler gear picks which spool to drive, the way a cassette deck does.
