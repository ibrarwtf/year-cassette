Turn clockwise to play forward (the crank winds the left spool). Turn anticlockwise to rewind (in the real box you turn the right knob instead).

Parts: 6 prints, no screws, no electronics

  1. Base tray. Two axle pins and two smooth guide posts are printed in, plus two springy brake tabs that rub the spool rims to keep the paper taut.
  2. Lid with a 124 × 64 mm window. It snaps onto the tray. Glue on a thin clear plastic sheet, or leave the window open.
  3. Spool × 2. Each has a 15 mm core with a slot for tucking in the paper end, and two 48 mm flanges.
  4. Crank, press-fitted onto the left spool's D-shaped shaft. Turning it plays forward.
  5. Rewind knob, press-fitted onto the right spool. Turning it rewinds.

How it works

  1. Tuck the start of the year's paper into the left spool's slot and the end into the right spool's slot. The whole year starts wound on the right.
  2. Turning the crank pulls paper across the window from right to left, so the days read left to right.
  3. The brake tabs drag lightly on whichever spool is unwinding, so the paper stays flat across the window.
  4. The paper slides over the guide posts on its back side only. The printed side touches nothing, because thermal paper darkens when rubbed.

Size

About 22 × 9 × 6 cm. A full year (365 squares, about 19.6 m of paper) rolls up to about 41 mm across. Every part fits a Bambu A1 or P1S bed.
